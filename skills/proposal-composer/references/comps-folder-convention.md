# Comps-Folder Convention — Keep Your Past Work Referenceable

The fee anchor is only as good as the history it can see. This skill is far sharper
when you keep a small, consistent folder of your past engagements and their outcomes.
It doesn't need to be elaborate — a folder of short notes, one per engagement, with a
few consistent fields — but the consistency is what lets the skill find the closest
comparable fast and anchor to a real number.

## The minimum viable comps folder

One note per engagement, past and pending, with a short, consistent frontmatter:

```
organization: <who>
format: <keynote | full-day | workshop | virtual | multi-session consulting | corporate>
audience: <who was in the room; size; sector>
fee: <the actual number, or TBD>
fee-notes: <ranges, add-ons, travel handling, anything the single number hides>
outcome: <booked / delivered / declined / ghosted>
date: <when>
```

Below the frontmatter: the session title(s) and description(s) you used, and any note
on how pricing landed (did they push back, what you traded, what you'd charge next
time).

## Why the fields matter

- **`format` and `audience`** are how the skill groups comps and finds the closest
  match — same shape, same kind of buyer.
- **`fee` as a single real number** is the anchor. Keep ranges and add-ons in
  `fee-notes` so the anchor stays clean.
- **`outcome`** lets the skill learn which numbers actually closed vs. which were
  declined — a fee nobody accepted is a weak anchor.
- **The session descriptions** are reusable raw material — the skill adapts the
  closest comp's titles and descriptions rather than inventing new ones.

## Grouping for the anchor

Keep enough history that each format has a visible band:

- virtual single session
- conference keynote
- full-day development (keynote + hands-on)
- multi-session / multi-week consulting
- corporate / private-sector (usually more budget latitude)
- custom multi-day program (top tier)

When an inquiry arrives, the skill pulls the band for its format and anchors to the
closest comp within it — adjusting up for larger-budget buyers (corporate, research
institutes), down for community or nonprofit.

## Hygiene

- **Real numbers only.** Never let a remembered or aspirational figure into `fee` —
  the whole value is that the anchor is something you actually charged.
- **Update outcomes.** A pending proposal becomes booked, declined, or ghosted; that
  status is signal for the next anchor.
- **One note per engagement, one canonical home.** Don't scatter the same engagement
  across files; the skill should find one authoritative record.
- **Keep it private.** This folder holds your real fees and terms — it's reference
  material for the skill, not part of any proposal that goes out.
