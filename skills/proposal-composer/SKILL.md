# Proposal Composer

Independent consultants quote in the dark: every proposal rebuilt from scratch, every
fee anchored to nothing but nerve. This drafts from your own history — past comparable
engagements, your bio, your session descriptions — and anchors the number to
precedent, with a private negotiation appendix you'll be glad exists. It drafts for
your review; it never sends.

## When to run it

On-demand, when an inquiry comes in or you're preparing a pitch. It works best once
you keep a small folder of past proposals and their outcomes (see
`references/comps-folder-convention.md`) — the more history it can see, the sharper
the anchor.

## Where it works

Works anywhere. In a file-capable tool it can read your comps folder directly; in
plain chat, paste the relevant past engagements and your bio.

## The procedure

The proposal structure is in `references/proposal-skeleton.md`; how to keep your past
work referenceable is in `references/comps-folder-convention.md`; teaching it your
voice is in `references/voice-profile-intake.md`.

1. **The ask.** Paste the inquiry. It extracts scope, audience, format, constraints —
   and what's conspicuously unsaid (budget hints, timeline, decision process).
2. **Comps pass.** Mine your past-engagements folder for the closest comparable work,
   grouped by format, so the fee band for each is visible. The real fees in those
   records are the anchors.
3. **Fee-anchored draft.** Assemble the proposal around a defensible number — stated
   with its anchor ("in line with your [comparable] engagement at that level") — in
   your voice, with your bio and session descriptions adapted from the closest comp.
4. **Negotiation notes.** A private appendix: your floor, your walk-away, and what to
   trade instead of price (scope, add-ons, travel handling) if they push back.

## Rules

- **Anchor to real numbers; never invent a fee.** The recommended number always
  traces to a comparable past engagement. If there's no comp, say so and give a
  reasoned range rather than a confident figure from nowhere.
- **Drafts only — never sends.** The fee is a grounded recommendation; you finalize
  and send. It never contacts the prospect.
- **Separate fee from expenses.** Keep the professional fee distinct from travel/
  lodging; follow the closest comp on whether to bundle or itemize.
- **Flag the entity/liability choice.** If the engagement could be signed personally
  vs. through your business entity, flag it — signing as an individual can undercut
  the liability and tax protection the entity exists for.
- **Voice, not boilerplate.** It sounds like you (see the voice profile), not like a
  proposal generator. No hype, no filler.
