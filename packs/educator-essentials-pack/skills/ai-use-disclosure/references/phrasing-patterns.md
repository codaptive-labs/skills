# Phrasing Patterns — Ready Disclosure Language

Drop-in phrasing, organized from lightest to fullest. Pick the smallest that's honest
for the actual use and the venue, then tailor the specifics. Bracketed parts are for
the user to fill.

## One-line notes (light, permitted use)

- "AI assistance ([tool]) was used for proofreading and formatting; all content and
  analysis are the author's."
- "Drafted with AI assistance and reviewed and edited by [name]."
- "[Tool] was used to suggest revisions to my own draft; I made the final decisions."

## Short statements (moderate use)

- "I used [tool] to brainstorm approaches and to critique early drafts. The
  structure, analysis, and final writing are my own. I reviewed all AI-suggested
  changes before accepting them."
- "[Tool] assisted with language editing and with organizing an outline I then wrote
  from. No factual content was taken from the tool without independent verification."

## Contribution logs (substantial, documented collaboration)

```
AI use on this work:
- Ideation: used [tool] to generate candidate angles; selected and developed [which] myself.
- Drafting: [tool] produced a first draft of [sections]; I substantially revised — [what changed].
- Editing: [tool] suggested line edits; I accepted [roughly what share] after review.
- Verification: [claims/citations] were checked independently (see audit) / were not AI-sourced.
- Not used for: [what the author did entirely themselves].
Accountability for the final work rests with [name].
```

## Formal / journal author-statements

- "During the preparation of this work the author(s) used [tool/version] to [specific
  role, e.g., improve readability and language]. After using this tool, the author(s)
  reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the
  content of the publication."
- Adapt to the target publisher's required wording; when in doubt, be more specific
  about the tool's role rather than less.

## Workplace / client lines

- "This document was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by [name/role] before
  delivery."
- "AI tools were used for [research synthesis / first-draft / formatting]; findings
  and recommendations were verified by the team."

## Choosing and tailoring

- **Match formality to venue** (see `disclosure-norms.md`), and **match length to
  actual use.**
- **Name the role, not just the tool.** Replace every generic "used AI" with what it
  did.
- **Cut anything untrue.** If the author didn't verify, don't say "verified" — say what
  actually happened, or go verify first (pair with Source Auditor).
- **Keep the human accountable.** Every fuller statement ends with who takes
  responsibility.
